Programs you can study in China

🎓 Diploma Program

SSC / Dakhil
  • Who can apply: SSC / Dakhil completed
  • Scholarship idea: SSC 4.5–5.0 → Tuition + Hostel Free (often)
  • SSC 3.0–4.5 → Tuition Free, Hostel 1000–2000 RMB (approx)
  • SSC below 3.0 → Partial support depends on university

🎓 Bachelor Program

HSC / Alim
  • Who can apply: HSC / Alim completed
  • HSC 5.0 + IELTS/Duolingo → Full Free + Monthly Stipend (strong chance)
  • HSC 4.5–5.0 → Full / Tuition Free (depends on subject & university)
  • HSC 3.0–4.0 → Partial (5000–10000 RMB/year approx)

🎓 Master’s Program

Bachelor CGPA
  • Who can apply: Bachelor completed
  • CGPA > 3.5/4.0 → Full Free + Monthly Stipend (often)
  • CGPA 3.0–3.5 → Tuition+Hostel Free / Only Tuition Free
  • Final depends on university ranking + subject + supervisor

🎓 PhD Program

Master’s + Research
  • Who can apply: Master’s completed (research profile helps)
  • Scholarship: Usually supervisor/university based
  • Higher chance with publications / research proposal
  • We help shortlist universities & supervisors
