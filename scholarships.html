❓ FAQ

Common Scholarship Questions

Short, clear answers — easy to scan.

Do I need IELTS for scholarship?

Not always. Some universities allow alternatives, but IELTS/Duolingo can help improve scholarship strength (especially for stipend options).

What documents are required?

Usually passport, transcripts/certificates, photo, and sometimes CV, recommendation letters, and language proof (English/Chinese).

Is scholarship guaranteed?

No. Scholarship depends on profile, university policy, intake competition, and available quota. We help maximize your chance through correct matching + clean submission.
